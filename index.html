A pill's journey through the body

  1. Mouth and esophagus. The pill is swallowed and reaches the stomach.
  2. Stomach. Acid dissolves the coating; some pills have an acid-resistant coating on purpose.
  3. Small intestine. Most of the active ingredient is absorbed into the blood here.
  4. Liver. Part of the substance is broken down before it reaches the general bloodstream.
  5. Bloodstream. The substance is carried to organs and tissues where it acts.
  6. Kidneys. Leftovers and breakdown products leave the body in urine.
